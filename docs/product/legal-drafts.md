## Terms of service — `terms-v1`

Talent DNA AI is an educational and experimental MVP. Users must provide accurate information, protect their credentials, and only upload content they have the right to use. Skill suggestions and matching outputs are guidance and do not guarantee employment or acceptance. MVP functionality may change during development. Harmful use and attempts to gain unauthorized access are prohibited. Acceptance is recorded as `terms-v1`.

## Privacy notice — `privacy-v1`

We collect basic account data, including name, email, and required session data. Passwords are stored as hashes, not readable text. In later stages, users may provide profile details, evidence, links, and assessment results. Data may be used to operate the account, extract potential skills, build user-reviewed results, and explain matching. Profiles and evidence are private by default, and analysis never publishes them automatically. Any future public publication requires explicit user action. Hosting providers and Brevo may process the minimum information required to operate the service and deliver email. The project does not claim to sell user data. Acceptance is recorded as `privacy-v1`. Account deletion is not implemented in this phase.
